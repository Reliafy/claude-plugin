# Life data in Reliafy

- Censoring flags, per time: 0 = the unit FAILED then; 1 = still running (right-censored, a suspension); -1 = left-censored (found failed then, failed at some unknown earlier time); 2 = interval-censored (failed between two times: the lower bound in data / time_column, the upper in data_right / time_right_column; other rows repeat their time there). Spreadsheets often use 1 = failed: pass c_invert=true rather than rewriting the data. A fit saying nearly every row is censored almost always means the flags are inverted; `censoring` gives the failures and censored units a fit used: check them against what the user said.
- Units already in service when records began are left-truncated (delayed entry): their entry age goes in trunc_left (trunc_left_column), not a flag. trunc_right is the age past which a failure couldn't be seen.
- Params are lists of {name, value} with SurPyval's names: weibull [alpha (scale), beta (shape)], exponential [failure_rate], normal / lognormal [mu, sigma], gamma [alpha, beta]; in the model's time unit.
- Fit and report: analyse fit (inline data, or dataset_id with time_column / censor_column). Keep it: change model_fit (same arguments plus name; inline data is saved as a dataset too). Fitted elsewhere (e.g. locally with SurPyval, the way when fitting isn't on the plan): change model_save with the distribution and params.
- distribution: weibull by default; 'best' fits every plain distribution and keeps the lowest AIC (criterion: aic_c, bic or neg_ll to rank by another score; selection gives each candidate's parameters, score and delta to the best, under 2 is a tie, what it skipped and why, and guidance in words: relay it; include_mixtures lets two-mode mixtures compete, decided by BIC); discrete, bounded, spline, regression (with covariates and a dataset: weibull_ph, weibull_aft, loglogistic_aft, weibull_po, weibull_ah, cox_ph, …; id_column for covariates that change over time; the read is a hazard ratio for _ph, a time ratio for _aft, an odds ratio for _po and an added failure rate for _ah; diagnostics.model_checks says whether the model's own assumption holds, with what to try if not), shared frailty (group_column; frailty_family lognormal for a heavier-tailed frailty), competing risks (causes / cause_column: each failure mode's share). method: MLE unless the user asks.
- A fit that didn't converge or has no finite maximum leads with fit_ok false: don't quote its numbers.
- Weibull beta < 1 = infant mortality, ≈ 1 = random failures, > 1 = wear-out.
- Read saved models: list kind models, get kind model (parameters with 95% CIs, goodness of fit, B-lives with one-sided 90% lower bounds; regression models: validation and the proportional-hazards checks).
- Evaluate: analyse reliability_at (R(t), F(t), hazard; conditional_age for a unit still working; confidence for bounds, quoted with the point values; covariates or a covariate_schedule for regression models). Bounds are null, with bounds_note saying why, where a model has none: never invent them.
- Requirements: analyse check_life_requirement ("B10 >= 50,000 at 90% confidence": meets only on the B-life's lower bound) and analyse plan_demonstration_test (units, test time and failures allowed; b_life, shape_model_id, producer_risk). Relay plan warnings. Other test plans: guide topic test_planning.
- One-shot items (a valve that must open): analyse fit_per_demand (batches of demands and failures; exact bounds); change model_fit with demand_batches saves one.
- "Is A better than B?": analyse compare_groups (split by a column or give a group per time; log-rank, RMST difference, Gray's test per mode, a verdict to relay). Nothing is fitted.
- Condition and life: remaining life from condition readings (a joint model, Pro): a reading taken again and again on each unit (vibration, wear, oil debris) and the units' lives, fitted together. One dataset (dataset_id) holding both: its life data, one row per unit (unit_id_column, age_column = age since new at failure or when last seen running, censor_column, optional entry_age_column and numeric covariates; age_right_column for units found failed between two ages, flag 2), and its measurements, one row per reading (reading_unit_column, reading_age_column since new, reading_value_column; change dataset_add_measurements puts them on the dataset: csv, an upload_id, or from_dataset_id to copy another dataset's table in). A dataset whose one table holds both (the flag filled in once per unit, a reading on every row) works as it is, or change dataset_split makes the two parts. Ages are since new, in one time unit (say it). hazard_covariates change the failure rate at a given reading; path_covariates change how the reading drifts; one may be in both. path_model: linear unless the readings clearly curve. analyse fit_joint reports; change model_fit_joint saves. The fit runs on the calculation service (8-40 s): it may come back as a job (get kind job). Quote each hazard ratio with its 95% CI, as its text says.
- A running unit's risk: analyse predict_joint (unit_id, or age_now + readings + unit_covariates): survival over the horizons with bounds, and the median remaining life. Never quote a mean life for these units: a reading that levels off can leave a chance of never failing, so the mean can be infinite; "not reached" means the median is beyond any finite age.

## The actions

Every call is `tool(action=…, args={…})` (`kind=…` for get and list), the action's arguments in `args`. `?` marks an optional argument; `guide(action='<tool>.<action>')` returns an action's full argument schema.

- `analyse(action='fit', args={distribution?=weibull, data?, data_right?, censored?, counts?, trunc_left?, trunc_right?, c_invert?, dataset_id?, time_column?, time_right_column?, censor_column?, count_column?, trunc_left_column?, trunc_right_column?, covariates?, method?, unit?, include_mixtures?, criterion?, frailty_family?, covariate_units?, cox_options?, id_column?, causes?, groups?, cause_column?, group_column?})`: Fit a life distribution (SurPyval) to inline times or a saved dataset and report it: parameters with 95% CIs, goodness of fit, metrics, and `censoring`, the failures and censored units the fit used (check them against what the user said).
- `analyse(action='fit_per_demand', args={batches?, dataset_id?, demands_column?, failures_column?, batch_column?, confidence?=0.95})`: Per-demand (one-shot) reliability: a valve that must open, a standby start.
- `analyse(action='reliability_at', args={model_id, times, conditional_age?, covariates?, confidence?, covariate_schedule?})`: A saved non-repairable model at given times: R(t), F(t), hazard, cumulative hazard and density; conditional_age adds the chance a unit that age survives a further t.
- `analyse(action='check_life_requirement', args={model_id, b_life, life, confidence?=0.9})`: Does a saved non-repairable model meet a B-life requirement ('B10 >= 50,000 cycles at 90% confidence')?
- `analyse(action='compare_groups', args={dataset_id?, time_column?, group_column?, censor_column?, count_column?, cause_column?, data?, group?, censored?, counts?, cause?, c_invert?, groups?, reference?, tau?, unit?, include_curves?})`: Is A better than B?
- `analyse(action='fit_joint', args={dataset_id?, unit_id_column?, age_column?, censor_column?, reading_age_column?, reading_value_column?, reading_unit_column?, age_right_column?, entry_age_column?, hazard_covariates?, path_covariates?, path_model?=linear, unit?, marker?, marker_unit?, c_invert?})`: Fit a condition and life (joint) model: how a reading drifts with age and how it raises the failure rate, from one dataset of life data with measurements; reports parameters with 95% CIs, hazard ratios and the riskiest running units.
- `analyse(action='predict_joint', args={model_id, unit_id?, age_now?, readings?, unit_covariates?})`: A unit's updating risk from a saved joint model: survival over the model's horizons with 95% bounds, and median remaining life ('not reached' when it never falls to a half — never a mean).
- `change(action='model_fit', args={name, distribution?=weibull, data?, data_right?, censored?, counts?, trunc_left?, trunc_right?, c_invert?, dataset_id?, time_column?, time_right_column?, censor_column?, count_column?, trunc_left_column?, trunc_right_column?, covariates?, method?, unit?, include_mixtures?, criterion?, frailty_family?, covariate_units?, cox_options?, id_column?, causes?, groups?, cause_column?, group_column?, demand_batches?, demand_confidence?=0.95})`: Fit exactly as analyse fit does and save the model (inline data is saved as a dataset too); returns its id and url.
- `change(action='model_save', args={name, distribution, params, unit?, dataset_id?, notes?})`: Save a non-repairable model from a distribution and parameters fitted elsewhere (e.g. locally with SurPyval: surpyval.Weibull.fit(x=times, c=censored), then its params), by SurPyval names, in the unit they were fitted in.
- `change(action='model_fit_joint', args={name, dataset_id?, unit_id_column?, age_column?, censor_column?, reading_age_column?, reading_value_column?, reading_unit_column?, age_right_column?, entry_age_column?, hazard_covariates?, path_covariates?, path_model?=linear, unit?, marker?, marker_unit?, c_invert?})`: Fit and save a condition and life (joint) model, as analyse fit_joint does, for analyse predict_joint and RBD blocks.
- `change(action='dataset_add_measurements', args={dataset_id, csv?, upload_id?, from_dataset_id?, sheet?, no_header?})`: Add or replace a dataset's measurements (readings: unit id, age since new, value) from csv, an upload_id or from_dataset_id (its table copied in).
- `change(action='dataset_split', args={dataset_id, unit_column?, age_column?, censor_column?, life_columns?, measurement_columns?})`: Split a dataset whose one table holds life data and readings (the flag filled in once per unit) into its two parts, in place; columns guessed.
- `get(kind='model', args={id})`: A saved model in full, life or recurrent.
- `list(kind='models', args={model_type?=all})`: Saved models: id, name, kind, distribution, fitted params, time unit (model_type: life or recurrent).
